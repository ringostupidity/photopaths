What does the score mean?

The score combines average image luminance and the proportion of dark pixels. It is a camera-based estimate, not a lux measurement. Exposure, weather, and different phones can change the result.

Camera and GPS require HTTPS or localhost. Images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded for measurements.
