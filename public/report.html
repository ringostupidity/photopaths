How reports help

1
Share what you see

A report appears on the map immediately, marked as unverified.

2
Check it together

Other visitors can confirm the problem or report that the light is repaired.

3
Add context to routes

Two confirmations mark a report as community corroborated. Nearby measured sections receive a small lighting penalty for recent corroborated reports.

4
Keep the map current

Two repair votes mark it community resolved. Votes are limited per browser, not verified identities.

Photopath does not send reports to your city. For repairs or urgent hazards, contact the responsible local service directly.
